## Framing, Paper & Placement Tips for US Kitchens

1. **Measure Your Coffee Bar Clearance:** An 18x24-inch print works well centered above a 24-to-36-inch espresso cart or open shelving; leave 6 to 8 inches above steam wands and grinders to protect the frame from moisture.
2. **Choose Matte Over High-Gloss Near Windows:** Matte or semi-matte archival paper cuts glare from under-cabinet lighting and kitchen windows.
