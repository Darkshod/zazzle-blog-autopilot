## Paper Stock & Layout Tips Before Ordering

1. **Select Matte or Felt Cardstock:** Uncoated matte or textured felt paper complements botanical line art and makes handwritten care notes on the back easy to read without ink smudging.
2. **Proof Phone Numbers & Instagram Handles Twice:** Use Zazzle's zoom preview to check kerning, margins, and bleed lines on both front and back.
3. **Use the Back for Care Instructions:** Add a brief *Trim stems at 45° and refresh water every 2 days* note so recipients keep your card near their vase.
