# Monetization & Compliance Guide (Morocco / Global Creators)

## 1. Zazzle Organic Product Sales
- Every article includes contextual product recommendation cards linking directly to your Zazzle product pages.
- Ordinary product links are labeled **"Direct Zazzle Product Link"**; affiliate links are labeled **"Affiliate Partner Link"** with `rel="sponsored noopener noreferrer"`.

## 2. Zazzle Ambassador / Associate Program (Morocco `MA` Verified Facts)
- **Eligibility:** Zazzle's Ambassador/Associate program allows global participation including **Morocco (`MA`)**. Self-promotion links (`?rf=<id>`) earn Creator Royalty + Self-Promotion Referral Commission; cross-promotion earns a base 15% referral commission.
- **Supported Payout Methods (Verified):**
  - **PayPal (`$50 USD` minimum threshold):** Primary supported electronic payout method. Ensure your Moroccan PayPal account is properly linked to receive and withdraw USD funds under local bank rules.
  - **Check by Mail (`$100 USD` minimum threshold):** Available, but **strongly discouraged** for Morocco due to international mail transit risks and bank cashing friction.
  - **Payoneer, Wise, Direct Bank Transfer (ACH/SWIFT):** **NOT natively supported by Zazzle.**
- **Payout Timing:** Earnings typically process ~45 days after the end of the month in which they were earned; balances below the threshold roll over.

## 3. Google AdSense Preparation
Required trust pages pre-built into `dist/`:
- `/about.html`
- `/contact.html`
- `/privacy-policy.html`
- `/editorial-standards.html`

Before applying for AdSense:
- Host on **Cloudflare Pages** (commercial monetization friendly) with a custom domain.
- Publish at least 15–25 original, helpful articles (`>= 750` words each) with zero thin/duplicate content.
